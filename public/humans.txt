/* NOTES */

  Invity Cards is a free digital invitation maker. You pick a design,
  type your event details, and get one link to share. Guests open it
  and RSVP in a single tap, with no app and no signup.

  No trackers run on an invitation page. If someone sends you a link,
  opening it sets no analytics cookie and builds no profile of you.
