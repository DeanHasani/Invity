About Invity

What Invity is

Invity is a free online invitation maker. You pick a design, type your event details, and get one shareable link — no printing, no app to install, and nothing for your guests to sign up for. They open the link, see your invitation, and RSVP in a single tap.

How it works

Build your invitation in four short steps: add the details, choose a design, pick a font, then set the animation. Hit create and you get a link you can send anywhere — WhatsApp, Messenger, email, or a group chat. Every RSVP lands in your guest list, and you get an email the moment someone responds.

Free and Pro

The free tier gives you two invitation links per day, and each link stays live for 8 hours. Invity Pro removes the daily limit, keeps links alive for 30 days, and unlocks the premium designs and effects plus CSV export for your guest lists. Pro is $2.99/month or $20/year, and it's backed by a 14-day money-back guarantee.
