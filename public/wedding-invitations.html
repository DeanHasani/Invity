Why a link beats paper for the small stuff

Paper invitations are wonderful for the ceremony itself — but nobody mails a card for the bachelorette party. A link reaches the whole group in one message, updates are instant, and responses collect themselves. Free accounts create two links a day (each live for 8 hours); Pro links stay live for 30 days — long enough to collect RSVPs across time zones and busy calendars, for $2.99/month or $20/year.
